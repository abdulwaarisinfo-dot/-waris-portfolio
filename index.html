Flintel —
Signal Intelligence
for Fintech & FX

This isn't a case study — it's a real, live product in production today, currently deployed with a Canadian fintech expanding to 25 markets. Flintel monitors Twitter/X, Reddit, Telegram, and competitor reviews around the clock. The moment a buyer posts publicly about a cross-border payment or FX problem your product solves, Flintel scores their intent, identifies who they are, drafts the outreach message, and delivers the full signal to Slack and HubSpot — in under 90 seconds.

  • Three-layer pipeline — Listener, Filter, Intelligence — with a two-stage filter that clears 95% of noise before AI ever touches it
  • Intent scoring 0–10 with buyer identification, routed automatically: instant alert, daily digest, silent watchlist, or discarded
  • AI-generated, ready-to-send outreach script attached to every qualified signal
  • Slack + HubSpot delivery in under 90 seconds — live in production, not a concept
  • Built for FX & remittance platforms, crypto exchanges, and B2B payments companies — anywhere buyers vent publicly before they switch

System Design Depth
8-Layer
Architecture

The AI E-Commerce Assistant isn't a wrapped ChatGPT call — it's a purpose-built retrieval intelligence system. Eight discrete layers handle everything from WebSocket transport to behavioral analytics, working in concert to deliver sub-second, contextually aware responses across Urdu and English.

01 — Transport

WebSocket Layer

Persistent bidirectional connections. Zero re-handshake overhead per message. Real-time streaming throughout sessions.

02 — Runtime

FastAPI Async Layer

ASGI non-blocking I/O. Concurrent session management without threading overhead.

03 — Language

NLP Query Layer

Urdu + English detection. Category, color, material extraction. Intent classification via pattern matching.

04 — Memory

Session Memory

Per-session state with multi-turn context. Preference history enables contextual follow-up generation.

05 — Retrieval

Product Retrieval

NLP signal → MongoDB filter translation. Querying against category, color, material, and price constraints.

06 — Scoring

Ranking Engine

6-dimension composite score: keyword relevance, category, color, material, rating, and trending weight.

07 — Telemetry

Analytics Pipeline

Behavioral event stream: searches, clicks, views, price signals, language patterns. Live feedback loop for calibration.

08 — UX

Dynamic Suggestions

Contextual follow-ups based on session state. 2–4 relevant refinements per turn guiding discovery flow.
