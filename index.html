Flintel —
Social Research
Reddit & Hacker News

People often say what they need before they ever talk to a vendor: "alternatives to…", "what do you use for…". Those posts are scattered and slow to triage. Flintel lets you search a brand, product, or topic in plain language and returns the matching conversations from Reddit and Hacker News, each linked to its source post and labelled by intent. It is built for founders, marketers, and researchers who want to read what people are actually saying before they act.

  • Each result carries a summary, source, date, sentiment, and an intent label (High, Medium, Low, or Uncertain) with a short reason
  • A strict definition of "qualified": High requires an explicit purchase step, and rows that can't be verified against the stored post are shown as Uncertain rather than inflated
  • Backend I build and maintain: background discovery and collection workers, LLM analysis with structured output, and a shared plus per-user MongoDB data layer
  • Free and paid plans with monthly search credits. Reddit and Hacker News are the live sources today; X, LinkedIn, and Facebook are listed as coming soon, and Flintel does not contact anyone on your behalf

Writora — Free AI Writing Assistant

Most people know what they want to say but struggle to phrase it in emails, replies, and messages. Writora takes rough text and returns a clearer version, with no account required. It is aimed at students, professionals, and non-native English speakers.

  • Six tools: improve writing, write a reply, change tone (14 options), simplify, shorten, and fix grammar
  • Dedicated pages for common tasks such as professional emails, follow-ups, and polite refusals
  • Each tool runs on its own task-specific instructions; text is sent to a third-party AI service and, per its privacy page, not stored
  • English works best today; other languages are listed as coming soon

System Design
Flintel
Architecture

Flintel is split into two services: a background engine that discovers and collects posts, and the user-facing web app that searches and presents them. Keeping collection and analysis out of the request path makes searches fast and lets long batch jobs recover from restarts.

01 — Collection

Discovery Engine

Multi-threaded background workers collect Reddit posts through Google SERP discovery and auto-generated fuzzy keyword matching.

02 — Analysis

LLM Intent Labelling

Claude/GPT scoring with function calling for structured output. Rows that can't be matched to a stored post are labelled Uncertain.

03 — Data

Shared + Per-User Cache

Async MongoDB (Motor) with a shared cache and per-user data. Batch processing is crash-safe and restartable.

04 — Product

Web App

FastAPI web app with plain-language search, source-linked results, search history, and credit-based plans.
